Vizag & Araku Highlights 2N/3D

The ultimate blend of coastal sea breezes and misty hill station serenity. Enjoy Rishikonda Beach, Submarine Museum, Kailasagiri Ropeway, followed by complete Araku Valley and Borra Caves exploration.

Tour Overview

Why choose between the beach and the mountains when you can enjoy both? The Vizag & Araku 2 Nights / 3 Days package is our most comprehensive signature tour. Start with the famous coastal city of Visakhapatnam exploring naval museums, hilltop ropeway vistas, and clean golden beaches before heading up into the cool valleys of Araku.

Ideal for families, vacationers, and first-time visitors to Andhra Pradesh looking for a well-rounded holiday with premium accommodations and seamless cab logistics.

3-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 • COASTAL VIZAG SIGHTSEEING

Submarine Museum, Kailasagiri & Rishikonda Beach

Pickup from Vizag railway station or airport. Check in to Vizag hotel. Visit the iconic INS Kursura Submarine Museum, TU 142 Aircraft Museum, take the Kailasagiri ropeway for hilltop panoramic sea views, and spend the evening enjoying speed boats at Rishikonda Beach.

DAY 2 • GHAT DRIVE TO ARAKU VALLEY

Borra Caves, Coffee Plantations & Resort Check-In

Post breakfast, embark on the winding mountain drive through Ananthagiri Ghats. Tour the subterranean marvel of Borra Caves, sample organic Araku coffee, check-in to your Araku resort, and unwind with an evening tribal bonfire and Dhimsa dance.

DAY 3 • VALLEY ATTRACTIONS & DEPARTURE

Padmapuram Gardens, Tribal Museum & Return

Morning visit to Padmapuram Botanical Gardens and the Araku Tribal Cultural Museum. Enjoy local shopping for authentic Araku spices, wild honey, and coffee beans before the return drive to Vizag for evening drop-off.
